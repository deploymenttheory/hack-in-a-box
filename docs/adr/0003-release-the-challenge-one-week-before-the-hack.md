# Publish the challenge seven calendar days before the live start

The theme and challenge brief are published to all confirmed attendees seven calendar days before the scheduled live start, at the same local clock time in the event's timezone. This gives teams a bounded period in which they may research, plan, set up tools and begin building their competition entries. Access before that common publication remains privileged and subject to the [competition eligibility restriction](0001-exclude-organisers-from-competing.md).

Publication requires the event to be in preparation, meet its minimum attendance, and have every confirmed attendee assigned to an organiser-confirmed team whose membership has been frozen. If the scheduled publication is blocked or missed, the organiser must postpone the hackathon to preserve the full preparation week or cancel the event.

An exceptional disruption after publication may justify postponement with explicit organiser approval and a recorded reason. Teams may continue working, the challenge remains available, and the revised live dates and submission deadline must be communicated to everyone. This is an explicit exception to the seven-day preparation window.
