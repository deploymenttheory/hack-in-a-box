# Form and freeze teams before challenge publication

The target team size is five competitors, with four to six preferred. Before team freeze, a team falling below three competitors has its remaining members reassigned to other hack teams; an organiser may explicitly approve rebalancing existing teams and forming new ones to accommodate displaced competitors.

Team freeze is a hard cutoff before the theme and challenge brief are published. No team reassignment is permitted after team freeze.

After team freeze, withdrawals may leave a team with fewer than three competitors. The team may continue with its remaining members or withdraw; the under-three reassignment rule applies only before the freeze.

If a team disbands, its repository is simply unused and unscored. No special work-transfer workflow is required.
