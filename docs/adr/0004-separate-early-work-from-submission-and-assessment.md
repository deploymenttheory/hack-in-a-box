# Separate early work from submission and assessment

Teams may begin building their competition entries and saving code or working drafts after challenge publication. Formal competition submissions open at the scheduled live start, and competition outputs are not accepted for review or scoring before then. This allows advance work while keeping review within the live hackathon.

During live work, mentors may provide feedback and provisional checkpoint scores. Final judging uses frozen submissions after the submission cutoff; the assessment stage may take place later on the final hack day and does not require a separate day.

The shared submission deadline freezes final submissions even if an organiser advances the stage later. An organiser may move the event from live to assessment only after that deadline, once every team has either a frozen final submission or an explicit not-submitted or withdrawn outcome. Missing submissions therefore have an explicit outcome and do not hold up assessment for other teams.
