# Gate preparation on minimum attendance

An organiser must explicitly advance an event from registration to preparation; the transition requires the event's configurable minimum number of confirmed attendees and closes new registrations. This makes preparation a deliberate commitment to proceed with sufficient confirmed competitors, rather than allowing preparation to overlap an open application window. Reaching the minimum only makes the transition available and does not advance the event automatically.

If withdrawals take attendance below the minimum during preparation, preparation continues with a visible shortfall. Withdrawals do not automatically cancel the event or move it backwards.

Existing waitlist offers remain valid and further FIFO offers remain possible during preparation until the final admission cutoff, under the approved automation and offer-expiry rules in the [waitlist admission policy](https://github.com/deploymenttheory/hack-in-a-box/issues/55). An unaccepted offer reserves capacity but does not count toward minimum attendance.

The final admission cutoff must be no later than team freeze, which precedes common challenge publication, so all competitors have a team and receive the full preparation week. Vacancies after that cutoff cannot be filled from the waitlist.

The minimum remains mandatory before challenge publication. For withdrawals after publication, an organiser may explicitly override the minimum for the transition to live with a recorded reason. This keeps an event proceeding after its go decision and protects the work teams have already invested; without an approved exception, the minimum still applies.
