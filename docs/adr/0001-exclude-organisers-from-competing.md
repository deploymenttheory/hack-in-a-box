# Exclude organisers and people with privileged advance knowledge from competing

Anyone who has held an organiser role for an event, or been granted privileged access to its unreleased theme or challenge brief, is ineligible to compete in that event even after the role or access is revoked. Advance knowledge creates an advantage that removing assessment permissions or revoking access cannot undo. Eligibility depends on the role or access grant, without requiring proof that the person opened or downloaded the material.

Granting an organiser role or privileged early access is blocked while the person has an active attendee registration, including a waitlisted registration. The person must explicitly withdraw before the grant, with the lasting loss of competition eligibility explained beforehand; a permission change must not silently cancel their place.
