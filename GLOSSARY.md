# Hack-in-a-Box

The people and competition concepts used when preparing, running and following up the organisation's hackathon events.

## Language

**Person**:
An individual whose identity is shared across events.

**Registration**:
A person's request for an attendee place at a particular event. Having a registration does not by itself make the person an attendee.

**Attendee**:
A person with a confirmed place to compete in a particular event. Holding an organiser, facilitator or assessor role does not by itself make someone an attendee.
_Avoid_: Participant

**Minimum attendance**:
An event's configurable floor of confirmed attendees. Waitlisted people and non-competing roles do not count toward this minimum.

**Admission offer**:
An invitation for a waitlisted person to accept an attendee place, temporarily reserving that place. An unaccepted offer is not a confirmed attendee place.

**Team**:
A group of attendees at one event working together on a single competition entry.

**Team reassignment**:
The movement of an attendee from one team to another within the same event.

**Team freeze**:
The cutoff before challenge publication after which attendees cannot be reassigned to different teams. Withdrawals remain possible.

**Competition entry**:
A team's contribution to the competition at a particular event. Only one competition entry is permitted per team per event.

**Challenge publication**:
The common point when an event's theme and challenge brief become available to all confirmed attendees.
_Avoid_: Release (without specifying what is being released)

**Live start**:
The scheduled date and time when the live hackathon begins. Teams may already have been working on their competition entries since challenge publication.

**Final submission**:
The version of a team's competition entry presented for final assessment.

**Submission cutoff**:
The shared deadline at which teams' final submissions are frozen for assessment.

**Mentor review**:
Feedback and provisional checkpoint assessment of a team's work during the live hackathon, used to guide the team and record progress.

**Final judging**:
Assessment of a team's frozen final submission after the submission cutoff.
